    The test itself

    What the Illinois examiner grades

    Before you drive

    • Car check: brakes, headlights, taillights, brake lights, signals, plate light, horn, wipers, mirrors, seatbelts. No cracked windshield.
    • Seat, mirrors, seatbelt adjusted before moving. Examiner buckled too.
    • Show the controls when asked: horn, lights, signals, hazards, wipers, defroster, parking brake.

    Required maneuvers

    • Straight backing about 50 ft, slowly, looking over your right shoulder (not mirrors alone).
    • Turnabout using an alley on your LEFT: left signal before entering, back out, stay on your side.
    • Uphill and downhill parking at the curb, and hill starts (where the site has a hill).
    • Parallel parking is not a standard part of the Illinois test, but the Parallel parking lesson covers it in case your site asks.

    During the drive (10 to 20 min)

    • Complete stops at every stop sign and red light.
    • Obey every sign, light and lane marking. Speed at the limit, never over.
    • Right-of-way: pedestrians, oncoming traffic, cross traffic, driveways.
    • Turns: signal 100 ft before, correct lane in and out, no wide or cut corners.
    • Lane changes and merging: mirror, signal, shoulder.
    • Smooth braking, steering and acceleration. Both hands on the wheel. Scanning mirrors.

    Automatic fail

    • Any traffic violation: rolling stop, speeding, red light, failing to yield (today's), wrong lane, no signal.
    • Any dangerous action, or the examiner has to grab the wheel or brake.
    • Hitting a curb hard, a car, a cone or a sign.
    • Passing a stopped school bus. Driving around a railroad gate.
    • Seatbelt off, or phone in use.

    What each light means

    Where exactly to stop

    1. At the white stop line.
    2. No stop line? Stop before the crosswalk.
    3. No crosswalk? Stop at the point nearest the cross street where you can see traffic, before entering it.
    4. Leaving a driveway, alley or parking lot: stop before the sidewalk, then creep up and stop again if you can't see.
    5. Railroad with lights or gate: 15 to 50 ft from the nearest rail.

    Full stop means the car stops rolling completely. Count 1-2-3. A rolling stop fails the test.

    Who goes first

    • Pedestrians in a crosswalk or on a sidewalk you cross: always them. Illinois says STOP, not just slow down.
    • Plain green, turning left: oncoming traffic.
    • All-way stop: first to stop. Tie: car on the right. Left turn yields to straight.
    • Leaving a driveway or lot: everyone on the street.
    • Roundabout: cars already in the circle (from your left).
    • Stop sign where cross traffic doesn't stop: cross traffic.
    • No signs at all: car on the right.
    • Emergency vehicle with lights and siren: pull right and stop. Clear an intersection or roundabout first.

    Every turn, same order

    1. Mirror check
    2. Signal at least 100 ft before (200 ft on faster roads outside town)
    3. Blind-spot check (bikes on the right!)
    4. Slow down before the turn, not during it
    5. Stop where required. Yield.
    6. Turn into the nearest lane going your way
    7. Make sure the signal shuts off

    School bus

    • Yellow flashing: bus is about to stop. Slow down.
    • Red flashing + stop arm: STOP before reaching the bus. Stay stopped until lights stop and the arm folds.
    • Two-lane road: traffic in BOTH directions stops.
    • Divided road or 4+ lanes: only traffic going the same direction as the bus stops.
    • Never pass a stopped school bus. Big fine, license suspension, and an instant fail.

    Railroad crossing

    • Slow down, look both ways, listen. Turn the radio down.
    • Lights flashing or gate down: stop 15 to 50 ft from the nearest rail.
    • Never drive around a gate. Never stop on the tracks.
    • Don't enter if traffic ahead could leave you stopped on the tracks.
    • Crossbuck only (no lights): slow, look, listen; stop if a train is coming.

    Speed limits in Illinois

    • 20 mph school zone, school days 7 am to 4 pm, children present.
    • 30 mph in town unless posted. 15 mph in alleys.
    • 55 mph other roads, 70 mph interstates unless posted.
    • Going over the limit at all is a violation. Going much slower than traffic is a mark too.
    • Construction zones: posted limit, fines double, workers present or not.

    Emergency vehicles and Scott's Law

    • Lights and siren behind you: signal right, pull to the right edge, stop. Not in an intersection.
    • Don't follow a fire truck within 500 ft.
    • Scott's Law: any vehicle stopped with flashing lights (police, tow, road crew): change lanes away if safe. If you can't, slow way down.

    Road lines

    • Yellow lines separate opposite directions. White lines separate same-direction lanes.
    • Solid yellow on your side: no passing. Broken yellow: passing allowed when clear.
    • You MAY cross a solid yellow to turn left into or out of an alley, driveway, private road or side street, or to get around an obstruction when clear.
    • U-turns: only with no sign against it, 500 ft of view, no curve or hill. Chicago: generally not at or near intersections or downtown. On the test, use the turnabout.
    • Double solid white: stay in your lane.
    • Center lane with arrows both ways: left turns only, never for driving.
    • Wide white bar: stop line. Striped bars: crosswalk.

    Parking distances

    • 15 ft from a fire hydrant
    • 20 ft from a crosswalk
    • 30 ft from a stop sign, yield sign or traffic light
    • 50 ft from a railroad crossing
    • Never in a crosswalk, on a sidewalk, in front of a driveway, or next to another parked car (double parking)

    Parking on a hill

    • Downhill: wheels turned toward the curb.
    • Uphill with a curb: wheels turned away from the curb.
    • No curb (up or down): wheels turned toward the edge of the road (right).
    • Parking brake on. Leaving: signal, mirror, shoulder check.

    Hand signals

    • Left turn: left arm straight out the window.
    • Right turn: left arm out, bent up at the elbow.
    • Slow or stop: left arm out, bent down.

    Other habits the examiner checks

    • Seatbelt on before the car moves. Mirrors and seat adjusted first.
    • Both hands on the wheel. Smooth braking and steering.
    • Follow 3 seconds behind the car ahead. More in rain.
    • Headlights on from sunset to sunrise and whenever wipers are on.
    • Pass a bicycle with at least 3 ft of space.
    • Phone off and away. Hands-free only, and not on the test.
    • Scan: mirrors every 5 to 8 seconds, look far ahead, check intersections left-right-left.
